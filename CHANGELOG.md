- **2026-08-08** — Fixed a live delevel bug affecting 8 of 12 characters on the server: Angel deleveled from 40 to 5 immediately after gaining experience from a normal kill in Oasis. Root cause: whatever prior process leveled these characters set `character_data.level` directly without setting a correspondingly correct `character_data.exp` — the moment any of them gained real experience, the server's own `SetLevel`/`SetEXP` logic correctly recalculated their true level from the (far too low) stored exp and deleveled them accordingly. Verified live via direct query; Angel confirmed showing level 40 in the online-character listing post-fix. (No ADR — direct character-data bug fix, not a design decision.) **Process note for future manual leveling:** always set `exp` to `GetEXPForLevel(level)` in the same operation as setting `level` directly, never one without the other.
