# Spells

## Status

Player spells and disciplines through Velious were comprehensively audited in
ADR-009. The baseline spell-data replacement is ADR-004; subsequent cosmetic
Necromancer illusion adjustments are ADR-012. Current behavior verification
belongs in `docs/development/MECHANICS_REVIEW.md`.

Use this document for future cross-class spell policy, rather than duplicating
the data ledger or ADR decisions.
