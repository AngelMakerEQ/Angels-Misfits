# NPC Reconciliation: Active Zone Review Queue

**Scope:** Classic, Kunark, and Velious only, with `zone.version = 0` and
`spawn2.version = 0`. Later zone versions are excluded as revamps.

## Ranking Basis

This is the initial loot-era queue, ranked by the number of active spawn
points that can reach one of six source-confirmed post-Velious items:

- Gates of Discord: Raw Indigo, Amber, Crimson, and Shimmering Nihilite.
- Secrets of Faydwer: Alkalai Loam and Flawless Spinneret Fluid.

The numbers are discovery priorities, not a claim that every NPC at a spawn
point drops every listed item. Shared lootdrop inspection remains mandatory
before a content-flag change.

## Review Order

1. Apply the source-backed Phase 1 content-flag draft only after manual
   review and backup; it addresses all six known item types wherever they
   occur, including the ranked zones above.
2. For each zone, inventory all remaining reachable loot items, then append
   only source-backed post-Velious items to a later content-flag phase.
3. After loot containment, compare ordinary NPC identity, spawn context,
   faction, combat fields, spells, and behavior against P99 or another
   field-appropriate classic source.
